Four Seas is a four-dimensional wavetable oscillator in Eurorack format. Its four related outputs inspire fun, creative patching, and happy accidents.

Four Seas has no screens, no menus, no presets, and minimal modes, making it an intuitive and playable instrument. Less RTFM and more music.

Features and functionality

Navigate through a sonic ocean using X, Y, and Z position to explore wavetables as a three-dimensional cube. You can expand your position with X, Y, and Z spread, allowing four different timbres to emerge, each at one of the four related outputs. You have twelve wave cubes at your disposal, stored on the SD card and fully user-replaceable.

The eight spread modes and a bipolar spread control let you spread the frequency of the four related outputs. This takes you from gentle detuning to lush chords and complex microtonal frequency relationship adventures. The four frequencies again emerge at the four related outputs.

The powerful modulation section features thru-zero phase modulation, phase distortion waveshaping, and bitwise XOR for complex sound design. Dual sync inputs with hard sync, phase shifting, and flip sync—selectable per output pair—allows for interaction with other oscillators. Each oscillator pair can also switch to LFO rates for modulation duties.

You can expand upon the functionality of the module with audio output pins and an I2C bus, enabling integration with Teletype, Crow, ER-301, and other compatible modules.
